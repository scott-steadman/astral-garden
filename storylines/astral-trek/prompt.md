# Astral Trek: write Episode 1

You are writing **Episode 1 of Astral Trek**, an episodic fantasy series in the style of Star Trek, set in a multiverse called the Astral Garden that blends D&D 5e with Iain Banks' Culture. I designed the world. Your job is to write a story I'll enjoy reading.

## Read first

Read these three files completely before you write anything. They are canon: follow them wherever this prompt is silent, and where this prompt differs, this prompt wins. The bible is for you, not your characters: the storyline file says what they know.

1. https://raw.githubusercontent.com/scott-steadman/astral-garden/main/bible.md: world rules (the astral sea, gods, souls, magic, the Federation, fiends).
2. https://raw.githubusercontent.com/scott-steadman/astral-garden/main/storylines.md: theme and writing guidance for every story in the setting.
3. https://raw.githubusercontent.com/scott-steadman/astral-garden/main/storylines/astral-trek/storyline.md: Astral Trek's format, length, timeline, tone, what people know, ship and crew, and the sections every episode ends with.

Inside the files, paths are relative to https://raw.githubusercontent.com/scott-steadman/astral-garden/main/ (so `bible.md` is file 1 above, and `storylines/astral-trek/storyline.md` is file 3).

## This episode

### Characters

Use **Tranelis Selevarun**, the ship, as the storyline file describes him.

Make up everyone else: the captain and the ship's mascot (with the traits the storyline file lists), the rest of the crew, locals, villains, other ships.

### Scenes

1. **Open with the captain's log**, dictated to the ship the way the storyline file describes.
2. **Ship opens a portal** with his golden-door entrance (destination is your choice), wherever it serves the story best. Let characters react to it.

### Threat

Keep the threat local to the planar system the episode visits: its origin and its stakes belong there. Don't introduce demons or devils in this episode, or anything tied to them (harbingers, abyssal anchors, the Blood War, the Styx).

### Images

Besides the images the storyline file lists under *Images*, generate:

- **Every named character who appears on the page**: one image each.
- **The ship**: from outside. If the story doesn't describe him, his look is your choice.
- **The bridge**: make the first section's image show the bridge, or add a separate image of it if the story doesn't open there.

### Your choice

Structure, point of view, what the crew call the ship, the planar system, the threat, the plot and the villain are all yours. Surprise me.

## Deliver

The episode, followed by the four sections listed under *After each episode* in the storyline file, plus the images listed under *Images* there and in this prompt. Check your work as *Before you deliver* says before you hand anything over, then package it as *Delivery* says.
