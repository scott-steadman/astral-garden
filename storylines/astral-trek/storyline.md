# Astral Trek: Storyline Setting

The episodic storyline in the Astral Garden. Shared world rules live in `../../bible.md`; this file holds what is specific to Astral Trek.

## Format

- Star Trek-style episodes. Each is self-contained, **up to an hour's read** (at most about 15,000 words), with a light series arc.
- Structure: Teaser, Acts One to Four, Tag. Episodes usually open with a captain's log to set things up, but that is a habit, not a rule; vary it when another opening works better. Logs can also bridge acts and carry exposition.
- Point of view (each storyline chooses its own; see `storylines.md`): **lower decks.** Arili and Away Team One carry most of the action; the captain and Ship are the big figures above them, with occasional captain-led scenes.
- Tone: serious stakes, warm crew, regular humor (Kono, Ship, knock-knock jokes). Not grimdark.
- Blood War (storyline-specific): in Astral Trek, **devils and demons are known** to the Federation, but **the Blood War between them is not**. Glimpses of it can come gradually; Episode 1 doesn't need to show it.
- Quiet circle (storyline-specific): the crew and Ship don't know it exists. The audience may glimpse it, for example in a cold open.
- Possible scene type: ships talking to each other over a conference call about what they've discovered (good cold open).

## The ship: *Tranelis Selevarun*

- Ensouled vessel; formerly a sea-elf bard (he/him). **Very young**: he died young and is new to being a ship, eager and a bit of a show-off; not a quiet-circle member. His bard level, then and now, is not yet decided. Speaks aloud and plays music instead of using telepathy. Calls the captain "Oh Captain, my Captain." Loves people asking about his music. Plays *Orlalassar's Battle Theme* (all action) and *Oribella's Theme* (tragic undertone). Keeps bawdy songs in his tavern by request. Likes to make an entrance: his portals open as a point of light that hisses, stretches into a line, then a rectangle, then a tall ornate golden door (Ep. 1).
- Runs most operations himself, so the bridge is usually empty except for the captain; full complement only for coordinated operations.
- Locations: bridge (outer ring of stations facing a central **scryer**), captain's ready room, council chambers, briefing room, tavern, great hall, gymnasium, druid grove, workshops, barracks, quarters, Beiwaren's shrine. Stone floors.
- Goes silent when shaken (he stopped playing when Quoor attacked).

## Crew

**Captain Elyerith Indimel** (she). Elf paladin. The Kirk. Straight-backed military bearing. Senses alignment by smell: good is clean like air after rain, evil is sulphur, the Abyss is rot and furnaces. Summons her sword by clasping her hands and calling "*Telantes!*"; it vanishes with a pop. Studies religions as a hobby. Numbers her away teams plainly (Team One, Team Four), true to her military bearing; the nicknames, like "Anjel's Devils", come from the crew. Dry humor (knock-knock jokes to Ship's drone). Strategic: sets traps by making things look ordinary.

**Arili Savore** (she). Gnome arcane trickster rogue and tinkerer; promoted from Crewmate to Ensign in Episode 1 and permanently assigned to Away Team One. Built the swappable **travel rod** case in Ship's drones: each rod holds one charge (a shift or a teleport) for whatever stands in its field, which is how she gets the team into Zozzoluz's chamber in Ep. 1. Point-of-view lead. Joined the Fleet out of boredom ("to go where no gnome had gone before"); was first drawn by the uniforms, since standing out borders on rude among gnomes. Sharp eyes, sees in the dark. Fights by tumbling, flanking, and backstabbing; uses caltrops. Thinks in mechanisms. Designed the quick-swap drone case for the Mark Five travel rod.
- **Manus**: her spectral mage hand, gray glove, perched on her right shoulder. Expressive (twiddles fingers, conducts invisible orchestras, salutes). Can fist up and punch.

**Kono** (he). Faerie dragon, ship's mascot. Gossamer wings flapped for show. Hunts the ship's rodents ("that was just an appetizer"). Calls Arili "gnomie."

**Commander Kysxaris Norralei** (she). Half-elf monk, first officer. Serene, unreadable face. Wants to learn to captain. Runs briefings and coordinates away teams from the ship.

**Security Chief Irla** (he). Half-orc fighter. Broad, wears his uniform tight to intimidate.

**Chief Religionist Ena Beiwaren** (she). Elf cleric; pendant of her god on a chain. Speaks with the dead, with mixed feelings. (Spelled "Beiwarin" in places in the original drafts; Beiwaren is used.)

**Chief Arcanist Ellie Ianthe** (she). Human wizard; gold circlet with glowing silver embroidery. Identified the Abyssal Anchor.

**Chief Tinkerer Hevin Nightglow** (she). Gnome artificer; tool harness so well kept nothing rattles. Arili's department head.

**Arch-druid Romain**. Tends the ship's grove; practices archery with Hiltrude.

### Away Team One, "Anjel's Devils"

- **Lieutenant Kimberly Anjel** (she). Elf fighter with amaranthine (dark) skin, lives in the gym. Decisive; wants candid feedback and expects her people to tell her when she's wrong. Says "Got it?" and counts down on her fingers. Armor glows on command. (Drafts vary: outline says drow, character list says human, text says amaranthine-skinned elf. Elf is used.)
- **Ensign Zanji Miggledy** (she). Gnome wizard, "wizard extraordinaire." Filigreed wand in a sewn pocket; eyelash-ball for invisibility; loves logic puzzles from the academy. (The outline cast Zanji as the Spock-type arcanist.)
- **Ensign Hiltrude Undertree** (she). Halfling ranger and scout; archer, short-sword, ash-and-sprig spell to muffle sound, can neutralize poison.
- **Ensign Arastruna Greatbrand** (she). Dwarf cleric and team healer; insignia glows when she heals; uses healing patches.

Other teams: Two and Three (standby, reinforcement), Four and Five (assault with local forces).

## Illithus (Episode 1's planar system)

- Sunless prime plane; one great city built over vast caverns. (Food source: to be decided; the troglodyte livestock is dropped.) Ruled by the **Elder Brain**, which is telepathic to about five miles and senses minds in that range.
- Gods: **Ilsensine** (missing; its home plane intact but ravaged) and **Maanzecorian**, god of secrets (slain on its home plane, apparently on behalf of Orcus; the plane is decaying). The Elder Brain hid Maanzecorian's death from its people.
- Illithids: about 5'6", purple rippled hide, domed heads, face tentacles over the mouth, three claw-tipped fingers and a thumb, levitate, telepathic to ~120 feet, pronoun "it." The Elder Concord includes Minder Quoor (killed), Creator Tharcerelin, and formerly Nourisher Saardsult. Prime Venerator Serisk keeps the Grotto of Sacred Thoughts and the Hall of Whispers; scripture is stored in encephalic fluid.
- Relations: Illithus will trade with the Federation but will not join.

## Villains and recurring figures

- **Orcus**, the bloated demon prince of the undead; has "unfinished business" with Maanzecorian. Sponsor of Zozzoluz.
- **Zozzoluz**: babau-type demon, 7+ ft, gaunt, gray, lipless, curved horn ending in a spike (the horn may be a tentacle with an eye). Killed in Astral Trek Ep. 1; demons killed off their home plane are banished back to the Abyss and reform (bible: Souls, Banishment), so it may return to Illithus once its banishment ends.
- **Saardsult**: ambitious illithid; cursed by Zozzoluz's dying breath into the first **ulitharid**, cut off from the Elder Brain. Fled Illithus with 41 followers to "rule elsewhere." Possible future: it spreads cursed illithid colonies across the planes. Recurring villain candidate.
- **The Illithus anchor** (Ep. 1): black iron and red stones; also summons demons. Captured and sent to Fleet HQ. Tied to something on the other end, still there.
