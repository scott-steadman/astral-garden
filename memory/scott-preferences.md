# Scott's preferences

- **"Your pick"**: Scott often answers setting questions this way. Offer one recommended option; they'll defer or override.
- **Story comes first.** Try to keep stories playable as D&D, but never let playability limit a great story. Scott would rather adapt a great story into an adventure later.
- **Decisions land in the files.** Setting decisions go into the matching topic section of the bible (astral sea, Gods, Souls, Magic, Federation, Fiends). Check the item off in `todo.md`.
- **To-do list**: `todo.md` holds Scott's open decisions and review passes. Keep it current.
- **Point of view**: Scott's earlier preference for Astral Trek was lower decks (Arili and Away Team One). On 2026-10-25, in the Episode 1 prompt, Scott left point of view, structure and most characters (all but Ship and Kono) to the writing AI.
- **Favorite scene**: Ship (Tranelis) opening the golden-door portal to Illithus.
- **Writing-coach notes** Scott values: every scene advances the story and is interesting on its own; sounds and smells, especially in character introductions; short sentences for pace; keep flashbacks and introspection brief.
