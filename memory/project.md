# Project: goal, plan, status

- **Owner**: Scott (private project).
- **Goal**: AI writes fiction Scott wants to read, set in the Astral Garden, a world Scott designed (D&D 5e mashed up with Iain Banks' Culture).
- **Origin**: Scott wrote an Astral Trek short story in the setting that didn't work overall but has scenes they like. They supplied an outline, a pre-review draft, and a coach-revised final (see `../notes/source-uploads.md`). Those were reworked into the bible, the storyline file, and the Episode 1 draft.
- **Plan** (Scott, 2026-10-11/12): finish the bible, do final review passes (bible, Astral Trek storyline), then completely rewrite Episode 1, keeping only Scott's favorite scenes. The current Episode 1 draft is not sacred; don't polish it, and treat its details (e.g. Arili's travel rod) as disposable.
- **Status (2026-10-31)**: storylines pass done; bible pass and Astral Trek pass still open (`../todo.md`). On 2026-10-25 Scott wrote `../storylines/astral-trek/prompt.md` so any AI can write Episode 1 from the hosted files.
- **Hosted copies**: Scott hosts the main files at https://www.stdmn.com/astral-garden (`/bible.md`, `/storylines.md`, `/storylines/astral-trek/storyline.md`) for other AIs to read.
- **Possible future use**: Scott runs D&D campaigns and may ask to turn stories they like into adventures for their own games.
