# Astral Trek

- **Format**: episodic, Star Trek style; each episode up to an hour's read. Default structure Teaser, four acts, Tag, with captain's logs for exposition (a habit, not a rule).
- **Files**: `storylines/astral-trek/storyline.md` (format, ship, captain's habits, Kono, arcs, end-of-episode sections) and `storylines/astral-trek/prompt.md` (the Episode 1 prompt).
- **Clean slate** (Scott, 2026-10-31): everything from Scott's original story (its crew, Illithus, its villains, the old Episode 1 draft, notes on the source PDFs) was removed. Don't bring it back; it survives only in git history (commit `374f321` and earlier).
- **Kept from it**: Ship's full profile (Tranelis Selevarun), Kono, and the captain's habits (knock-knock jokes, plainly numbered away teams, smelling alignment) for whatever captain Episode 1 invents.
- **Episode 1**: Ship and Kono are fixed; the writer invents everyone else. Required scenes: an opening captain's log, and Ship opening a portal with his golden-door entrance.
- **Prompt vs storyline** (Scott, 2026-10-31): `prompt.md` is given to an AI by URL and holds only what is specific to the episode (characters, scenes, overrides). Rules that hold for every episode (length, timeline, captain's log, the three end sections) live in `storyline.md`; theme, craft, terminology and D&D guidance live in `storylines.md`.
- **Arcs** (Scott, 2026-10-31): multi-episode arcs live in the *Arcs* section of `storyline.md`; an episode prompt names the arc it belongs to, or none for a standalone.
- **After each new episode**: record kept characters, the episode and any arcs in the storyline file, and move nothing into the bible unless Scott declares it canon.
