# Astral Trek

- **Format**: episodic, Star Trek style; each episode up to an hour's read. Episodes usually open with a captain's log (a habit, not a rule); structure is otherwise the writer's choice.
- **Files**: `storylines/astral-trek/storyline.md` (format, what people know, ship, captain and mascot traits, arcs, end-of-episode sections) and `storylines/astral-trek/prompt.md` (the Episode 1 prompt).
- **Clean slate** (Scott, 2026-10-31): everything from Scott's original story (its crew, Illithus, its villains, the old Episode 1 draft, notes on the source PDFs) was removed. Don't bring it back; it survives only in git history (commit `374f321` and earlier).
- **Kept from it**: Ship (Tranelis Selevarun; Scott trimmed his profile on 2026-11-02, so he has a sense of humor, perhaps witty repartee with the mascot, and the rest of his personality is the writer's, settled by the Episode 1 winner; his age and appearance are chosen only if relevant to the story) and traits for whatever captain and mascot Episode 1 invents (Scott, 2026-11-02: human female paladin who rarely focuses to smell alignment; a flying, talking, snarky mascot who hunts rodents and nicknames the crew). Kono is no longer canon.
- **Episode 1**: Ship is fixed; the writer invents everyone else. Required scenes: an opening captain's log (dictated to Ship), and Ship opening a portal with his golden-door entrance.
- **Prompt vs storyline** (Scott, 2026-10-31): `prompt.md` is given to an AI by URL and holds only what is specific to the episode (characters, scenes, overrides). Rules that hold for every episode (length, timeline, captain's log, the three end sections) live in `storyline.md`; so do the storyline's D&D-abilities rule and quiet-circle setting (it doesn't exist yet). Theme, craft and terminology live in `storylines.md`.
- **What characters know** (Scott, 2026-11-02): the first draft knew too much of the bible, so the storyline file now lists what's known, disputed and unknown in year 1, and metadata has a *Bible facts revealed* row. The Federation doesn't know about planar drift in this storyline, even though the bible's chart format includes it.
- **What the crew call Ship** (Scott, 2026-11-02): a storyline choice (Ship, name, initials, ...); "Ship" in the files is shorthand. The Episode 1 winner decides, then record it in the storyline file.
- **Levels** (Scott, 2026-11-02): ships' and characters' levels are never stated in the story. They're a private guide to a being's power and 5e abilities, used only when relevant; only the *Character details* end section may mention them.
- **Logs** (Scott, 2026-11-02): the captain dictates her log to Ship, who may add a comment. There is no logbook or twin at Fleet HQ; how logs are stored or sent is up to the writer, if relevant. Crew can dictate private logs to Ship. Ship's own log is an occasional option, not the opening.
- **Arcs** (Scott, 2026-10-31): multi-episode arcs live in the *Arcs* section of `storyline.md`; an episode prompt names the arc it belongs to, or none for a standalone.
- **After each new episode**: record kept characters, the episode and any arcs in the storyline file, and move nothing into the bible unless Scott declares it canon.
