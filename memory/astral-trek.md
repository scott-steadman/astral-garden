# Astral Trek

- **Format**: episodic, Star Trek style; each episode up to an hour's read. Default structure Teaser, four acts, Tag, with captain's logs for exposition (a habit, not a rule).
- **Files**: `storylines/astral-trek/storyline.md` (format, ship, crew, tone, open threads after Episode 1), `storylines/astral-trek/episode-01-the-drifting-world.md` (current draft, Illithus first contact), `storylines/astral-trek/prompt.md` (prompt for the rewrite).
- **Fixed characters for the rewrite**: Tranelis Selevarun (Ship, ensouled former sea-elf bard, very young) and Kono (faerie dragon mascot). Everyone else is the writer's choice per the 2026-10-25 prompt; the storyline file still describes the original crew.
- **Required scenes for the rewrite**: opening captain's log written by hand and stowed in the captain's chair (twin logbook at HQ updates); Ship opening a portal with his golden-door entrance.
- **Storyline-only facts**: the Federation knows devils and demons but not the Blood War; the crew and Ship don't know the quiet circle exists. Year 1 of the Federation; drones, no avatars yet.
- **Prompt vs storyline** (Scott, 2026-10-31): `prompt.md` is given to an AI by URL and holds only what is specific to the episode (characters, scenes, overrides). Rules that hold for every episode (length, timeline, captain's log, the three end sections) live in `storyline.md`; theme, craft, terminology and D&D guidance live in `storylines.md`.
- **Arcs** (Scott, 2026-10-31): multi-episode arcs live in the *Arcs* section of `storyline.md`; an episode prompt names the arc it belongs to, or none for a standalone.
- **After each new episode**: update the storyline file's open threads, and move nothing into the bible unless Scott declares it canon.
